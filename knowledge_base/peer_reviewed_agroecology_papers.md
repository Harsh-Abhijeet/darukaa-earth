# Synthesized Peer-Reviewed Agroecological Literature

## Section 1: Multi-Metric Interactions of Soil Organic Carbon and Water
By Lal R. (Ohio State Carbon Management and Sequestration Center):
- Carbon-Water Multiplier: Every 1% increase in soil organic carbon (SOC) in the top 30 cm increases plant-available water holding capacity (AWHC) by 1.5% to 3.7% by volume, translating to an additional 20,000 to 45,000 liters of water stored per hectare.
- Drought Buffering: In semi-arid regions receiving 400-600 mm annual rainfall, soils with SOC > 1.2% preserve green crop canopy cover for 12 to 18 days longer during acute mid-season dry spells compared to degraded soils (SOC < 0.5%).
- Yield Response: Restoring depleted cropland soils by elevating SOC by 1 Mg C/ha/year increases dryland grain yields by 20 to 70 kg/ha for wheat and 10 to 50 kg/ha for legumes under water-constrained conditions.

## Section 2: Diversified Farming Systems and Ecosystem Services
By Kremen C., Miles A. (Ecology Letters):
- Biocontrol Services: Crop diversification via strip-cropping or intercropping increases beneficial parasitoid and predator abundance by 44% (95% CI: 32%-58%) and reduces herbivorous pest damage by 31% without prophylactic insecticide application.
- Pollinator Synergy: Farms maintaining multi-species floral borders alongside monocultures demonstrate a 38% increase in native bee visit frequency and a 22% increase in fruit set for pollinator-dependent crops.

## Section 3: Regenerative Organic Amendments and Biological Inoculation
By Pretty et al. (Nature Sustainability):
- Biochar and Compost Synergies: Co-application of matured compost with pyrolyzed biochar (5-10 t/ha) in degraded neutral-to-alkaline soils increases soil cation exchange capacity (CEC) by 18-35% and microbial biomass nitrogen by 30-50% over a 2-year window.
- Quantitative Uncertainty Clause: Quantitative yield improvements depend heavily on regional precipitation patterns, soil texture (sand vs clay buffering), and baseline nutrient exhaustion. For specific microclimates where localized multi-year trial data is missing, precise yield projections cannot be responsibly guaranteed.
