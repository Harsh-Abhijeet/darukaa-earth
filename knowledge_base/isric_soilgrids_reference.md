# ISRIC SoilGrids Reference & Environmental Interpretation

## Section 1: Standard Soil Property Thresholds
ISRIC provides standardized global soil depth intervals (0-5cm, 5-15cm, 15-30cm, 30-60cm, 60-100cm, 100-200cm). For environmental diagnostics and biodiversity modeling, the top 0-30 cm represents the critical bio-active zone.

Interpretation Matrix:
1. Soil Organic Carbon (SOC in dg/kg or wt %):
   - Very Low: < 0.5% (< 5 g/kg) - Acute risk of desertification and structural breakdown.
   - Low: 0.5% - 1.0% - Degraded biological function, low water retention.
   - Moderate: 1.0% - 2.0% - Adequate for staple crops, moderate macrofauna support.
   - High: > 2.0% (> 20 g/kg) - High biological buffer capacity, high aggregate stability.
2. Soil pH (in H2O 1:5):
   - Strongly Acidic: < 5.2 - High risk of aluminum toxicity; low biological nitrification.
   - Moderately Acidic: 5.2 - 6.0 - Reduced availability of P, Ca, Mg.
   - Optimal: 6.0 - 7.3 - Peak bioavailability of primary nutrients and microbial diversity.
   - Alkaline / Calcareous: 7.4 - 8.4 - Zinc and iron chlorosis risk; phosphorus lockup.
   - Sodic / Strongly Alkaline: > 8.5 - Dispersion of clay particles, severe soil sealing.
3. Bulk Density (fine earth in cg/cm3 or g/cm3):
   - Uncompacted (< 1.25 g/cm3): Optimal root exploration and earthworm porosity.
   - Moderate (1.25 - 1.45 g/cm3): Normal agricultural soil.
   - Restrictive (> 1.55 g/cm3 in clays, > 1.65 g/cm3 in sands): Root penetration resistance exceeds 2.0 MPa, physically impeding plant roots and macrofauna.
